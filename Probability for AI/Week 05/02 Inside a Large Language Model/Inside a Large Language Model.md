### Example 01

This lesson began with a question: where does GPT-2 keep its facts? For example, how does it know that the Louvre is in Paris?

Now that you have seen inside it, what do you think?

**Answer:**

I think GPT-2 keeps its facts in the weights (parameters) of the neural network. During training, it learns patterns and relationships from huge amounts of text. For example, it learns that the words "Louvre" and "Paris" are strongly connected.

It doesn't store facts like a book or database. Instead, the information is spread across many connections in the network. When asked a question, it uses what it has learned to predict the most likely answer.
