## What's your winning chance?

At a school fair, you get to participate in **two independent lucky draws**. The probability of winning a prize in the first draw is 40%. The probability of winning a prize in **at least one of the two draws** is 70%.

What's the probability of winning a prize in the second draw?

A. 30% \
B. 40% \
C. 50% \
D. 70%

#### **Answer:**  OptionC: 50%

---

**Explanation**

**The idea**
Subtract the overlap between the two independent events to avoid counting it twice

**Solution**

The draws are independent, so $P(A or B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A)P(B)$. With $P(A)=0.4$ and $P(B)=p$:

$0.7=0.4+p−0.4p$

so $0.3=0.6p$ and $p=0.5$. The second draw wins half the time.
