## At least one girl

A family has two children. Assume that each child has an equal probability of being a girl or a boy, independently of the other child. You learn that at least one of the children is a girl. What is the probability that both children are girls?

A. 1/2 \
B. 1/3 \
C. 1/4 \
D. 2/3

#### **Answer:**  OptionB: 1/3

---

**Explanation**

**The idea**
Information shrinks the event space

**Solution**

We know that one child is a girl. That leaves the following possibilities for the two children, in order of birth: (girl, girl) (girl, boy) (boy, girl)

and so P(girl, girl) = 1/3
