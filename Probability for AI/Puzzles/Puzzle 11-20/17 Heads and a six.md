## Heads and a six

You flip a fair coin and roll a fair die. What is the chance of getting **heads and a 6**?

#### **Answer:** 0.0833

---

**Explanation**

**The idea**
For independent events, "and" means multiply.

**Solution**
The coin and the die are independent, so multiply: \
1/2 x 1/6 = 1/12  ≈ 0.083.
