## Blind Box Surprise

A blind-box toy line has 10 different characters. The shop fills every box at random from a huge stock: each box is equally likely to hold any of the **10** characters, **independently of every other box**. So the same character can turn up again and again, like rolling a 10-sided die for each box.

You open five boxes, and none of them holds your favorite character. 😢 What's the probability that your favorite is in the next box?

#### **Answer:** 0.1

---

**Explanation**
 
**The idea**
A Miss Doesn't Change Your Odds!

**Solution**
Each box is a fresh, independent draw, so the five boxes you already opened don't change the next one: the chance your favorite is inside is 1/10.

If instead the boxes held 10 different characters with no repeats, five misses would leave 5 characters, and the answer would be 1/5. Here repeats are possible, so it stays 
1/10.
