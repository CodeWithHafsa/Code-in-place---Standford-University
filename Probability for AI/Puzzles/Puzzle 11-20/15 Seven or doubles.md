## Seven or doubles?

You roll two fair dice. Which is more likely: the dice add up to 7, or you roll doubles (both dice show the same number)?

A. A total of 7 \
B. Doubles \
C. They are equally likely

#### **Answer:** They are equally likely

---

**Explanation**

**The idea**
Count equally likely outcomes, not totals.

**Solution**
They are equally likely. Two dice have 6 x 6 =36 equally likely outcomes. Six of them add to 7: (1,6), (2,5), (3,4), (4,3), (5,2), (6,1). Six of them are doubles: (1,1) through (6,6). So each has probability 6/36 = 1/6.
