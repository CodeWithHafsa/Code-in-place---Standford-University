## Anything but six

You roll a fair six-sided die. What is the chance it does **not** land on 6?

![alt text](Images/image-3.png)

#### **Answer:** 0.833

----

**Explanation**

**The idea**
The chance something does not happen is one minus the chance it does.

**Solution**
Five of the six equally likely faces are not a 6, so the chance is 5/6 ≈ 0.833. Equivalently, 1 − 1/6.
