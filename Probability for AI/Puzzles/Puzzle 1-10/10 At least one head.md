## At least one head

You flip a fair coin three times. What is the chance you see at least one head?

#### **Answer:** 0.875

---

**Explanation**

**The idea**
When "at least one" has many cases, count the one case that fails.

**Solution**

The only way to get no heads is TT, which has chance 1/8 (one of 8 equally likely outcomes). Every other outcome has at least one head: 1 - 1/8 = 0.875.
