## Exactly one head

You flip two fair coins. What is the chance you get exactly one head?

A. 1/4 \
B. 1/3 \
C. 1/2 \
D. 2/3

#### **Answer:** 1/2

---

**Explanation**

**The idea**
Make sure your outcomes are equally likely before you count them.

**Solution**
There are four equally likely outcomes: HH, HT, TH, TT. Two of them have exactly one head, so the chance is 2/4 = 1/2.

It is tempting to say 1/3 (zero, one, or two heads), but those three are not equally likely: "one head" can happen two ways.
