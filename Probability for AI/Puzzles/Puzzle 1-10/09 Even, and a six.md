## Even, and a six?

A friend rolls a fair die and tells you it landed on an even number. What is the chance it is a 6?

#### **Answer:**0.333

---

**Explanation**

**The idea**
Conditioning shrinks the world to the outcomes that are still possible.

**Solution**
Knowing the roll is even leaves three equally likely faces: 2, 4, and 6. One of them is a 6, so P (six | even) = 1/3.
