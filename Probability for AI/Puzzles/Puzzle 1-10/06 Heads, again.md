## Heads, again?

A fair coin has landed **heads five times in a row**. You flip it once more.

What is the chance the sixth flip is heads?

A. Less than 1/2: tails is due \
B. Exactly 1/2 \
C. More than 1/2: heads is on a roll

![alt text](Images/image-5.png)

#### Five heads in a row. What comes next?

#### **Answer:** Option B: Exactly 1/2

---

**Explanation**

**The idea**
A fair coin has no memory.

**Solution**
Exactly 1/2. Each flip of a fair coin is **independent** of the others: the coin has no memory of the last five, so the next flip is heads with probability 
1/2, as every flip is.

The feeling that tails is "due" is so common it has a name, the gambler's fallacy.
