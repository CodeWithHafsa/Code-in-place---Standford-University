## The Faculty Placement Puzzle

**Dr. Samina** and **Dr. Ali** are organizing their teaching schedules. Out of their classes, **Dr. Samina** teaches 1 High School class and 3 Graduate classes, while **Dr. Ali** teaches 2 High School classes and 3 Graduate classes. A student is placed into one of these 9 classes at random, each equally likely. **Given that the chosen class is at the High School level, what is the probability that Dr. Samina is the instructor?**

A. 1/4 \
B. 1/3 \
C. 1/2

![alt text](Images/image-40.png)

#### **Answer:**  OptionB: 1/3

---

**Explanation**

**The idea**
Finding conditional probability by narrowing your total choices down to just one matching group.

**Solution**

First, restrict your sample space to only the total number of High School classes taught by both instructors to serve as your new denominator (1 + 2 = 3). Then, divide Dr. Samina's High School classes (1) by that total to find the final conditional probability of **1/3**.

**P(Dr. Samina | High School) = (Dr. Samina's High School Classes) / (Total High School Classes)**
