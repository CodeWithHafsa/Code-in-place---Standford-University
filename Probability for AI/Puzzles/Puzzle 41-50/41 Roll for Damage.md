## Roll for Damage

A Blue Haze with 30 hit points attacks ten adventurers: 2 Elves, 2 Valkyries, 3 Warriors and 3 Wizards. Each adventurer rolls a D4 (4 sided die with numbers 1-4) to see how much damage they wreak upon the Blue Haze. Since there are 2 Elves and 2 Valkyries, they get to double their roll values. Each of the 3 Warriors and 3 Wizards triples their roll. The Blue Haze is defeated if the total damage is at least 30. What is the probability that the adventurers defeat the Blue Haze?

A. We all lose: You can't solve this problem with probability. 

B. There's no way the team could all defeat it with only D4s. They need more powerful dice!

C. It's possible but unlikely-- under 20% chance of victory.

D. They have a fighting chance, but I wouldn't bet on it: 20 - 50%

E. They have a decent chance. Overall probability favours our heroes, but they aren't out of the woods yet: 50 - 90%

F. Curtains for you, Blue Haze - over 90% chance the heroes win.

![alt text](Images/image-34.png)

#### **Answer:**  OptionF: Curtains for you, Blue Haze - over 90% chance the heroes win.

---

**Explanation** 

**The idea**
There are very few combinations that would total under 30 points, and many that total be more.

**Solution**

The worst they could roll would be all ones for a total of 26: 8 from the doubled pairs + 18 from the six tripling players. There's no way to get 27 total and only a few ways to get 28 (4 ways: one Elf or Valkyrie rolls 2; all others roll 1s) and 29 (6 ways: a Warrior or Wizard rolls 2, all others 1s). The rest of the many combinations are better rolls that defeat the Blue Haze.
