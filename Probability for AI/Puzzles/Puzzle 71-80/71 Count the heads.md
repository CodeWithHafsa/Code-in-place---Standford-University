## Count the heads

Flip three fair coins and let X be the number of heads. X can be 0, 1, 2, or 3.

Which value of X is the most likely?


A. 0 \
B. 1 \
C. 2 \
D. 3 \
E. 1 and 2 are tied

#### **Answer:** Option E: 1 and 2 are tied
---

**Explanation**

**The idea**
A random variable is a number that depends on chance; its distribution says how likely each value is.

**Solution**
There are 8 equally likely outcomes. One has no heads (TTT), three have one (HTT, THT, TTH), three have two, and one has three. So 
$P(X=1) = P(X=2) = \frac{3}{8}$: a tie.
