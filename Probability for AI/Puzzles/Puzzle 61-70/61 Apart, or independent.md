## Apart, or independent?

Two events A and B each have a positive chance of happening, but they **can never happen together**.

Can A and B be independent?

A. Yes, always \
B. No: knowing A happened tells you B did not \
C. Only when they are equally likely

![alt text](Images/image-50.png)

#### **Answer:**  OptionB: No: knowing A happened tells you B did not

---

**Explanation**

**The idea**
Never together is the opposite of independent.

**Solution**

No. Independence means P(B∣A)=P(B): learning A tells you nothing about B. But if A and B can never happen together, then once A happens, B is impossible: P(B∣A)=0, which is less than P(B). Learning A told you everything about B.
