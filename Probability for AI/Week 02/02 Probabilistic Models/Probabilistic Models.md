#### Question # 01
A story makes one family of eight people, oldest first: two grandparents, their two children, the two people those children married, and one kid for each couple.

Anyone whose parents are not in the family (the grandparents and the two who married in) carries a gene with chance $θ_1 = 0.05$. Everyone else can only get it from a parent: a parent who carries it passes a copy to each of their children with chance $θ_2 = 0.5$.

Which of these are the knobs (the parameters) of this model?

* θ₁ and θ₂: the chance a person carries the gene, and the chance a parent hands down a copy
* The eight people in the family
* The 10,000 families the story makes
* P(my cousin carries it | I carry it)

**Answer:** Option1: θ₁ and θ₂: the chance a person carries the gene, and the chance a parent hands down a copy

#### Question # 02

A story makes one family of eight people, oldest first: two grandparents, their two children, the two people those children married, and one kid for each couple.

Anyone whose parents are not in the family (the grandparents and the two who married in) carries a gene with chance $θ_1 = 0.05$.

Everyone else can only get it from a parent: a parent who carries it passes a copy to each of their children with chance $θ_2 = 0.5$.

Run the story once. What do you get?

* One family: for each of the eight people, whether they carry the gene
* The chance that my cousin carries the gene
* The values of θ₁ and θ₂
* The chance of every possible family

**Answer:** Option1: One family: for each of the eight people, whether they carry the gene
