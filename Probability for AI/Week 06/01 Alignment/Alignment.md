### Question 01
In this lesson, the rain machine said there was a 70% chance of rain tomorrow.

Imagine it says there is a 70% chance of rain on 100 different days. If the rain machine is well calibrated, about how many of those 100 days should have rain? Explain in a sentence or two.

#### Answer:

About 70 days should have rain out of the 100 days. A well-calibrated rain machine means that when it predicts a 70% chance of rain, it should rain on about 70% of those days.
